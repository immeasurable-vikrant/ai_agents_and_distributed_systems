The three things to actually try.
1. Memory: tell it your name, then ask "what is my name?" — it remembers, because the checkpointer reloaded the state for this thread_id.
2. Isolation: click + New chat and ask your name again. It has no idea. Different thread = different memory.
3. Durability: Ctrl-C the server, restart it, hit Reload from DB. The conversation is still there — it was never in the browser or in the process.
