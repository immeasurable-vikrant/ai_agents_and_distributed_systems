📊 Eval Harness

How do you know your agent works? Measure it — and catch the regression the average hides.
Click "Compare v1 vs v2". The overall score barely moves (-0.033) — the kind of delta you'd shrug off. But one test case drops -0.2 and the build is BLOCKED.

The cause: v2's prompt says "ALWAYS use a tool", so it calls get_weather on "Hello, who are you?". The answer is still fine — every output-based eval passes. Only the trajectory eval catches it.

That's the whole lesson: aggregate metrics hide regressions, and output-only evals miss process bugs.
